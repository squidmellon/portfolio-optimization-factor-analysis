# Limitations

- Portfolio construction and performance evaluation use the same sample.
- No holdout period or rolling out-of-sample test is included.
- Short selling is permitted without borrow constraints or fees.
- Transaction costs, taxes, and turnover are ignored.
- Mean-variance weights are sensitive to expected-return and covariance estimation.
- The stock universe contains only eight selected equities and is not representative of the full opportunity set.
- A statistically significant sample alpha does not establish underpricing or investment skill.
- The 0.025 Sharpe-ratio advantage over SPY is economically modest relative to the portfolio's additional volatility and implementation complexity.
