# Model interpretation

## Market regression
The regression places optimized-portfolio excess return on the left-hand side. The estimated beta is approximately **0.718**, with R-squared of **28.4%**. The monthly alpha is approximately **0.999%** and is borderline at conventional significance levels.

## Fama-French and Carhart models
The three-factor and four-factor specifications have R-squared values of approximately **2.1%** and **2.6%**. The included factors explain little of the optimized portfolio's monthly return variation in this sample.

The positive multifactor alpha should be treated cautiously. The portfolio was optimized and evaluated using the same observations, and the small stock universe can create idiosyncratic or omitted-exposure patterns that are not captured by the factors.
